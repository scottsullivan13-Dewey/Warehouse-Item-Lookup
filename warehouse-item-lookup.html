    Put it on your home screen

    Android: tap the browser menu ⋮, then “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).

    iPhone: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen.”

    It lands as “Item Lookup” with its own icon and opens full-screen — no browser bar, right from the floor.

    Opening this link from inside another app (like Muse)? Add it from the app and Android may stamp that app's icon on the shortcut instead. Open the link in Chrome first, then add it — you'll get the magnifier icon and the “Item Lookup” name.

    Adding a location? Tell Dewey the brand, part #, and shelf as you pick — the list here gets refreshed and this same page shows the new items, for you and for Frank.
